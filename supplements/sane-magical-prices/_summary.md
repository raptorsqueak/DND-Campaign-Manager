# Sane Magical Prices — Summary

**Slug**: sane-magical-prices
**Kind**: rules-supplement
**Added**: 2026-05-08
**Source**: Community-authored by Saidoro et al., compiled from /r/DnDNext, EnWorld, and Giant In The Playground forums. Original layout by SalmonSquire. References DMG page numbers for each item.

## What this is
A pricing guide for D&D 5e magic items that tries to make the magic-item economy internally consistent — replacing the wide-band DMG rarity-tier prices with specific gold values per item, sorted by price ascending. Intended as a relative-value reference, not a literal "every shop sells these" rule.

## Scope
Covers **317 priced magic items** across five categories:
- **Consumables** (63) — potions, scrolls, single-use tokens
- **Combat Items** (117) — weapons, armor, offensive/defensive items
- **Noncombat Items** (53) — utility items for exploration and social play
- **Summoning Items** (16) — items that summon allies
- **Gamechanging Items** (68) — high-impact items that reshape encounters or the campaign

Also lists ~20 items that are **intentionally not priced** (Wishes, Deck of Many Things, plot-device items) with one-line reasons.

## When to consult
- Any time the DM needs a price for selling, buying, or valuing a magic item
- When estimating treasure-hoard total value for an encounter or arc
- When a player asks "how much would this cost to commission?"
- When evaluating whether a homebrew item's price is in the right ballpark

## Overrides / conflicts
This supplement **replaces** the DMG's broad rarity-tier price guidance (DMG p. 135). It does not override any rules — only pricing. The DM is explicitly told (in the source) to adjust prices freely; treat the table as a starting point, not RAW. No conflicts with the SRD or other supplements.
