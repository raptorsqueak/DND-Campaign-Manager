## Magic Items / Mechanics

All priced items live in `content.md`, sorted by price ascending within each section. To look up a single item, grep the file by name. Each row has columns: **Item | Price (gp) | DMG Page | Rarity**.

### Quick price-bracket guide

| Bracket | Section likely | Example items |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ 100 gp | Consumables (low scrolls, basic ammo) | Spell Scroll Level 0, Potion of Healing |
| 100–1,000 gp | Mostly Consumables, some low-tier Combat | Potion of Speed, Vicious Weapon |
| 1,000–10,000 gp | Combat / Noncombat / mid-tier rings | +1 Weapon, Bracers of Defense |
| 10,000–50,000 gp | High-end Combat / Gamechanging | Holy Avenger, Vorpal Sword |
| 50,000+ gp | Legendary, Gamechanging only | Robe of Stars, Amulet of the Planes |

### Price-not-listed items

The "Items That Won't Be Priced" section enumerates ~20 items the source intentionally excludes (Wishes, randomness-based items, plot devices). Consult `content.md#items-that-wont-be-priced` if asked about: *Deck of Many Things, Ring of Three Wishes, Wand of Wonder, Bag of Tricks, Belt of Giant Strength, Helm of Brilliance, Staff of the Magi, Manual of Golems, cursed items*, and similar.
